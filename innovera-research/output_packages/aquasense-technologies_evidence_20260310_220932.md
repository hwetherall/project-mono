## Context Signals

**Problem:** Municipal water utilities across North America lose 20-30% of treated water through leaks in aging distribution infrastructure, costing an estimated $2.6 billion annually in non-revenue water in the US alone. Current detection methods rely on manual acoustic surveys by field crews covering only 2-5 miles per day, and most utilities operate reactively — discovering leaks only after visible damage or pipe failure. Regulatory mandates (EPA LCRR, California SB 555) are imposing compliance deadlines for water loss auditing, while an aging utility workforce exacerbates the operational challenge.

**Solution:** AquaSense Technologies deploys networks of IoT acoustic sensors on water mains that continuously monitor pipe vibration to detect leaks in real time without excavation. A cloud-based AI platform classifies leak severity, predicts pipe failure probability, prioritizes maintenance work orders by risk score, and integrates with existing SCADA and GIS systems. The platform also generates automated compliance reports for AWWA water audits, and is delivered as a hardware lease plus SaaS subscription.

**Target Customer:** Primary customers are mid-sized US municipal water utilities serving populations of 50,000–500,000 (approximately 1,200 utilities), which face significant water loss but lack resources for proprietary monitoring systems. Secondary targets include industrial facilities with critical water infrastructure such as food and beverage manufacturers, data centers, and pharmaceutical plants.

**Problem Keywords:** water pipe leaks, aging water distribution networks, non-revenue water loss, water main breaks, manual leak detection, pipe failure, water infrastructure investment gap, reactive maintenance, acoustic leak detection, municipal water utility operations, pipe condition assessment, water loss auditing, utility workforce shortage
