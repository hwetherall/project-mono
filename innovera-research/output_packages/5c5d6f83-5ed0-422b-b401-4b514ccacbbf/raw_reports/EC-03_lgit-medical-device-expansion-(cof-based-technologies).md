7. IMPLICATIONS FOR LG INNOTEK (LGIT)
==================================================================
• Capital availability exists: multiple $50-200 M rounds for companies scaling substrate fabs with medical certifications.  
• Tier-1 strategics are writing cheques and even taking majority stakes once technical risk is retired – a potential exit avenue that supports LGIT’s >20 % IRR target.  
• Market consolidation signals scarcity of ISO-13485 qualified flex substrate capacity – aligns with LGIT’s goal to hit $350 M rev. by Year 3; operating-margin benchmarks (Surmodics, Cirtec) run 28-34 %, validating LGIT’s 30 % OM assumption.  
• Rapid corporate-VC participation suggests that establishing early joint-development or supply agreements with OEMs (Medtronic, Dexcom, Boston Scientific) will de-risk capacity utilisation.  
• Recommendation: fast-track 10-k-class clean-room in either Austin (proximity to BCI cluster: Paradromics, Precision) or Minnesota (legacy Medtronic ecosystem) and pursue minority co-investment from an anchor OEM to mirror Blackrock/Tether precedent.
