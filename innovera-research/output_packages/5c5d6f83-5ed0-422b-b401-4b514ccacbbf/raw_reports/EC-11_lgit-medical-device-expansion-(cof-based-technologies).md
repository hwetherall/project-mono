────────────────────────────────────────
5. KEY TAKE-AWAYS
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Search interest for problem-area phrases (flex medical substrates, electrode arrays) is clearly up (30–60 % CAGR) from a small base; interest is concentrated in the US, Germany, Switzerland, the Netherlands and Japan—mirroring the MedTech R&D clusters.  
2. Job-posting volumes and unique specialist titles grew ~20 % YoY on major boards. Both OEMs and CDMOs are staffing fabrication, process, and quality roles, indicating the problem is being tackled internally but capacity is still constrained.  
3. The specific solution-category (medical-grade flex substrate CDMO services) shows the fastest relative growth in search—even though absolute query counts remain <300/mo. Early commercial language (“BCI CDMO”) is beginning to register, suggesting an emerging market category.  
4. Competitor head-count data show contract manufacturers adding personnel more aggressively than large OEMs, implying that supply-chain partners are scaling up ahead of expected demand in CGM, neuro-stim and BCI verticals.

Limitations & caveats:  
• Google Trends indexes relative, not absolute, search activity; low-volume B2B terms can be volatile.  
• Job-board scraping misses confidential or recruiter-only searches and over-represents English-language postings.  
• Some smaller CDMOs and stealth BCI start-ups do not post publicly; hiring counts therefore understate total demand.
