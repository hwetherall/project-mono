## Strategic Insights

### Venture Strengths
- **Specialized thin-film / fine-pitch substrate technology** (CoF, advanced flex circuits) that Tier 1 CDMO incumbents Flex and Jabil explicitly lack — defensible niche in high-density BCI, implantable electrode, and biosignal interface applications.
- **Proven precision manufacturing scale** — LG Innotek's quality systems heritage and OEM relationships in electronics provide a credible foundation for ISO 13485/FDA certification acceleration and rapid MedTech capacity ramp.
- **Low displacement risk from T1 incumbents** at the substrate/electrode component tier — Flex and Jabil operate at the device assembly layer and are more likely partners than direct competitors.

### Venture Weaknesses
- **No MedTech-specific regulatory credentials** — ISO 13485, FDA establishment registration, and ISO 10993 biocompatibility testing are all absent, creating a significant OEM qualification barrier.
- **Limited MedTech commercial track record** — no 'lock-in' dynamics or long-term supply agreements comparable to Flex/Jabil incumbency advantage.
- **Sparse competitive intelligence** — 16.7% data fill rate across 33 competitors; zero data on Integer Holdings (T1 incumbent in the exact target segment).
