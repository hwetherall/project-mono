7.  WHAT THE DATA SUGGESTS  
————————————————————————————————  
• Market is still EARLY-STAGE but maturing – average round size up from US$5-7 m (2022) to US$15-25 m (2024-25).  
• Corporate buyers are the most consistent liquidity path (Badger, Diehl, Watts, Resideo). That, in turn, pulls Tier-1 climate funds in at Series A/B stage.  
• Regulatory tailwinds (EPA draft rule on water-loss auditing, multiple state-level “SB555-style” mandates) are now explicitly cited in earnings calls and VC memos.  
• Competitive white-space: municipal network-scale leak prediction (vs. single-building devices) is still fragmented; no clear “category king.” Integration with existing AMI/SCADA stacks and operator workforce shortages remain key selling points called out by investors.
