# Venture Brief

CASE FRAMING MEMO
Innovera.ai PoC – Medical Device Expansion (CoF-Based Technologies)
________________________________________
1. Context
The company possesses advanced PCB and CoF (Chip-on-Film) substrate manufacturing capabilities, including fine-pattern photolithography and plating technologies capable of 10µm-level circuit formation on rigid and flexible substrates 
These capabilities enable:
•	Ultra-thin, flexible circuit formation
•	High-density, fine-pattern electrode structures
•	Multi-channel implementation on compact films
•	Enhanced device miniaturization and design flexibility
The company is exploring entry into high-value medical device markets, specifically:
•	Continuous Glucose Monitoring Systems (CGMS)
•	Nerve stimulation devices
•	Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) systems 
The strategic rationale is based on:
•	The industry shifts toward personalized, preventive, AI-enabled healthcare 
•	Growth in wearable monitoring demand
•	Increasing neurological and cardiovascular disease prevalence
•	Need for thin, flexible, high-density electrode substrates in implantable and wearable devices
LG Innotek (LGIT)  is evaluating whether its CoF-based substrate capabilities can be extended beyond fabrication into components, or module participation in the medical device value chain 
The company is not currently operating in the MedTech domain and is assessing, from a strategic perspective, whether to advance ongoing development efforts into a full-fledged business. For context, our primary business today focuses on packaging, optical, and mobility solution technologies serving the IT and automotive markets.

LGIT does not have its own CGM technology, nor does it have a development history of bio signal monitoring sensors for smartwatches. However, they are currently developing CGM sensor substrates/modules in collaboration with a major CGM company, and they are also in discussions with a leading neurostimulation company regarding joint development of neurostimulation electrodes. The company has more than five years of technical development experience in neurostimulation electrode technology through collaboration with a startup.
Their strategic focus is not on finished devices but on developing core components and modules for small wearable sensors, as well as key components and modules for implantable devices such as neurostimulation and BCI systems.

1.	The company is not pursuing an OEM end product business model; instead, they are considering a component supply or CDMO business.
1.	 The company’s strategic objective is to expand from substrates into modules and systems.
2.	M&A is permitted as part of the entry strategy: The company is also considering the acquisition of early stage startups with system solution capabilities like Echo. 
3.	In terms of  investment amount related to M&A, there is currently no specific amount under consideration; however, as a useful reference, LGIT recently made an investment of approximately USD 35 million.
4.	As our primary target markets are CGMS, neurostimulation, and BCI, the company intends to evaluate market entry while carefully considering the risks associated with implantable devices.
5.	Horizon for case modeling: The company intends to consider its business model both at the projected marketing point 3 years from now and the subsequent expansion strategy for the 3 more years following market entry.
6.	Echo partnership is in the exploration phase: The company has entered an NDA with Echo and is currently exploring collaboration at the development stage. As part of this PoC, we intend to assess Echo alongside other potential candidates to identify and select the most suitable strategic partner.
7.	Regulation approach: Entering the medical device business requires extensive preparation for various regulatory certifications. LG intends to verify in detail the medical grade material and component certifications we are familiar with (e.g., ISO 10993), as well as manufacturing facility certifications (e.g., ISO 13485). In addition, they seek to identify any certification requirements they may not yet be aware of and assess the potential business implications associated with them.

4. Success Criteria

Primary Objective 
Establish a defensible position in a high-growth medical device segment that:
•	Generates structural revenue growth beyond commodity substrate markets
•	Provides superior margin profile relative to core PCB/CoF fabrication
•	Strengthens long-term strategic positioning in advanced electronics

Financial Outcomes
1.	Revenue target: The company’s annual revenue target at the third year after market entry is approximately USD 350 million.
2.	IRR target: Our target for MedTech-related business is to achieve an IRR of over 20% and a payback period within three years.
3.	Acceptable margin: The company is not currently engaged in any MedTech-related business, and the operating profit target for MedTech-related business is set at over 30%. 
Strategic Outcomes
•	Expand capabilities from substrate manufacturing into higher-value participation (component/module/system)
•	Create optionality for expansion into adjacent medical verticals
Operational Outcomes
•	Avoid disproportionate regulatory burden relative to opportunity size
•	Maintain acceptable exposure to implantable-device liability risk
